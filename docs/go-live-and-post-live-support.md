# Go-live & post-live support

The rollout did not end at installation. I supported live operations at the branches, continued to resolve application incidents, and escalated issues to the relevant team or technology vendor where necessary.

## During go-live

- Helped branch employees adopt the application in active operations.
- Checked application functionality and responded to immediate blockers.
- Coordinated with relevant internal IT stakeholders and the technology vendor as required.

## After go-live

- Continued troubleshooting application incidents in the live retail environment.
- Supported employees as they used the POS system in daily work.
- Documented/followed up on issues and escalated problems outside my resolution scope.
- Supported continuity of branch operations through application support.

## Why this is called post-live support

**Post-live support** describes continued assistance after deployment. **Hypercare** usually refers to a special, time-limited period of heightened support, and is not asserted here because a formally defined hypercare arrangement was not established for this portfolio summary.

## Boundaries

This page contains no real incident tickets, logs, screenshots of internal systems, branch identifiers, sensitive configurations, access details or production data.
