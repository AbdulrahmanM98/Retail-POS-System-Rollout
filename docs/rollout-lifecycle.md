# Rollout lifecycle — Jeddah & Taif

This document expands the professional project narrative in the README. It is a reconstruction of the contributor's responsibilities, **not an internal employer project plan or approved corporate SOP**.

## 1. Initiation and assigned scope
The organizational initiative was a rollout of a retail POS application. My assigned geographic implementation scope covered retail branches in **Jeddah and Taif**. The project schedule was established and assigned to me; I was responsible for coordinating branch-level activities and executing work against that schedule. I did not create or own the overall enterprise rollout plan, budget, or governance structure.

## 2. Rollout coordination and readiness
I prepared for branch implementation, coordinated with branch employees, internal IT stakeholders and the vendor when needed, and checked readiness for installation and testing.

Readiness included relevant workstation/peripheral connectivity, application environment, access and operational prerequisites. The [readiness checklist](branch-readiness-checklist.md) is a generalized portfolio artifact, not a copy of employer documentation.

## 3. Installation and configuration
I installed/configured the POS application as assigned and checked whether the branch environment was ready for practical verification. My account of the work does not imply ownership of application development, enterprise architecture or vendor-managed components.

## 4. UAT and issue follow-up
I executed UAT scenarios, documented results, flagged defects or blockers, and coordinated resolution or escalation. Testing results were used to check that relevant branch transactions and workflows were ready for live operation. See [UAT overview](uat-overview.md).

## 5. End-user enablement
I personally delivered training to branch employees, helping them become familiar with the new POS application and the daily workflows relevant to their roles.

## 6. Go-live and post-live support
I supported employees during live operation and continued to troubleshoot application issues after deployment. Issues requiring another team or the vendor were escalated. This support continued beyond initial installation and therefore is described as **post-live support** rather than claiming a formally defined, time-limited hypercare phase.

## 7. Completion of assigned rollout scope
The POS rollout was completed for the **Jeddah and Taif scope** assigned to me. The project status does not imply that an entire nationwide rollout ended or that operational application support stopped.

## Scope and attribution
The POS solution and overall enterprise project were delivered by a wider set of organizational and vendor stakeholders. My contribution here is rollout coordination and hands-on implementation/support within my assigned branch scope.
